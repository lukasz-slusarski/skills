# Deadlines and enforcement (Poland)

As of 8.10.2026. Read when the question is about "since when", "who supervises", "how big are the fines" or content from before August 2026.

## Since when

- **2.08.2026**: Article 50 of the AI Act applies in full, including paragraph 4 (deepfake and text, a deployer obligation). Source: Article 113 of Regulation 2024/1689.
- **Digital Omnibus on AI** (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, published in the OJ EU on 24.07.2026, in force 27.07.2026; the number and dates come from secondary sources and the Commission page, the text of the provision from the official text PE-CONS 30/26 REV 1, https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/PE-30-2026-REV-1/en/pdf): a transitional period only for **providers** and only for **Article 50(2)** (machine marking) in systems placed on the market before 2.08.2026:
  > Providers of AI systems, including general-purpose AI systems, generating synthetic audio, image, video or text content, that have been placed on the market before 2 August 2026 shall take the necessary steps in order to comply with Article 50(2) by 2 December 2026.

  The deployer obligation under paragraph 4 has **no transitional period**. This is confirmed by Commission guidelines point 153 and the Commission FAQ: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act . The Omnibus also changes Article 50(7) (codes of practice) and does not change paragraph 4.
- **Content from before 2.08.2026** (guidelines point 154): generated and published earlier does not need a retroactive label. Generated earlier but published from 2.08.2026: label it.

## Poland: the Act on artificial intelligence systems

- Act of 3 July 2026 on artificial intelligence systems (ustawa o systemach sztucznej inteligencji), **Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1003** (Journal of Laws 2026, item 1003), promulgated 27.07.2026, Sejm print 2443. Text: https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1003/text.pdf . Status in ISAP/Sejm API: in force, effective 11.08.2026.
- Article 127: the provisions on inspection, proceedings and penalties (Articles 8-18, chapters 3-5, 8 and 9) enter into force **3 months after promulgation**, that is 28.10.2026. Until that date the Polish authority does not yet have procedural tools for inspections and penalties, but the Article 50 obligation applies anyway from 2.08.2026 (the regulation applies directly).
- **Supervisory authority**: Komisja Rozwoju i Bezpieczeństwa Sztucznej Inteligencji (KRiBSI, the Commission for the Development and Safety of Artificial Intelligence), Article 125(1). It cooperates among others with KRRiT (Krajowa Rada Radiofonii i Telewizji, the National Broadcasting Council; systems generating image, audio, video in media services and video platforms), the President of UOKiK (Urząd Ochrony Konkurencji i Konsumentów, the competition and consumer protection office) and the President of UODO (Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych, the data protection office) (Article 20(1)).
- **Penalties** (Article 104(1)): KRiBSI imposes penalties by decision "in the cases, amounts and on the conditions specified in Chapter XII of Regulation 2024/1689", that is for Article 50 up to EUR 15 million or 3% of worldwide turnover, for SMEs the lower of those amounts (Article 99(4)(g) and (6) of the AI Act). The Act has no separate rates and no provisions of its own on labeling AI content (search of the full text: none).
- Exclusion (Article 3(1)(1) of the Act): natural persons using AI in the course of personal, non-professional activity, consistent with Article 2(10) of the AI Act.

## Other Polish authorities

I did not find (8.10.2026) positions of UOKiK, KRRiT or UODO on labeling AI content in advertising or influencer marketing. The recommendations of the President of UOKiK for influencers (2022, https://uokik.gov.pl/download/26685) do not mention AI. Regardless of the AI Act, the ban on unfair market practices applies: an ad with a generated product or "customer" that misleads may be a violation of the Act on Counteracting Unfair Market Practices (ustawa o przeciwdziałaniu nieuczciwym praktykom rynkowym), also when labeled as AI.
