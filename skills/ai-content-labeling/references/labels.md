# Label format

How to label when the verdict is LABEL. Basis: Code of Practice, section 2, Commitments 1 and 3, Annex 1 (final version 10.06.2026, https://ec.europa.eu/newsroom/dae/redirection/document/129555) and Commission guidelines points 117 and 141-143. The Code is voluntary, but it is the best available pattern of a "clear and distinguishable" disclosure under Article 50(5).

## Icon

- Main element: capital letters **"AI"** (Measure 1.1 a). A national-language version is acceptable where language laws require it (in Poland the Polish Language Act: the safe solution is an "AI" icon plus Polish text next to it).
- Official EU icons (Annex 1), free to use without attribution to the Commission: **"AI + GENERATED"** for fully generated content, **"AI + MODIFIED"** for partly altered content, and the basic **"AI"** to be completed with your own text. In user tests the variants with text performed clearly better. Files: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eu-icons-labelling-ai-generated-content
- Any color and size, as long as it is legible, high-contrast and recognizable (Measure 1.1 c).
- Invisible metadata (C2PA, a watermark from the tool) is **not sufficient** (guidelines point 117).

## Where and when

| Form | Placement |
|---|---|
| Image | on the image, in a spot without other overlays, e.g. the top right corner (1.2.2 a) |
| Video, live | at the start, where possible at regular intervals and at least after breaks; best throughout the segment with the deepfake (1.2.2 b) |
| Audio | a short spoken notice at the start, in plain language; reminders for long or live content; where there is a screen, additionally the icon (Measure 1.1, 1.2.2 e, 1.2.3) |
| Text | above the text, next to the title or in an editorial footer at the beginning; only the fragment with AI may be labeled (1.2.2 f) |
| Artistic work, satire, fiction | icon next to the frame, in the description, in opening or closing credits; visible without clicking (Commitment 3) |

Always: at the latest at first exposure, without requiring a click or expansion, visible long enough. Information only in terms and conditions, in a tab or under "more" in a description is insufficient (guidelines points 141-143). In social media posts and on YouTube the description is often collapsed, and text under "more" is not visible without clicking, also for works: for a deepfake, an icon on the image or video itself is safer than a hashtag.

## Ready-made texts

Use the language of the audience; local language laws may require it. The English texts below are the primary wording. The Polish texts are kept for publications in Poland: the Polish Language Act is the reason to give the label in Polish there.

### English

- Fully AI image: "AI GENERATED" icon + in the description "Image generated by AI."
- Image or video altered by AI: "AI MODIFIED" icon + "Altered using AI: [what, e.g. background, face, voice]."
- Voice clone, audio: at the start of the recording "This voice was generated by artificial intelligence." For a clone of your own voice: "You are listening to my AI-generated voice."
- Avatar in video: a title card at the start + an icon in the corner: "Presenter generated by AI."
- Text on public matters without human editing: above the text "Text generated by AI." or "This article was prepared by AI."
- Work, satire: in the description or credits "Contains scenes generated by AI."

### Polish (PL)

- Obraz w całości z AI: ikona „AI GENERATED” + w opisie „Obraz wygenerowany przez AI.”
- Obraz lub wideo zmienione przez AI: ikona „AI MODIFIED” + „Zmienione przy użyciu AI: [co, np. tło, twarz, głos].”
- Klon głosu, audio: na początku nagrania „Ten głos został wygenerowany przez sztuczną inteligencję.” Przy klonie własnego głosu: „Słuchasz mojego głosu wygenerowanego przez AI.”
- Awatar w wideo: plansza na początku + ikona w rogu: „Prezenter wygenerowany przez AI.”
- Tekst w sprawach publicznych bez redakcji człowieka: nad tekstem „Tekst wygenerowany przez AI.” albo „Ten artykuł został przygotowany przez AI.”
- Utwór, satyra: w opisie lub napisach „Zawiera sceny wygenerowane przez AI.”

Additionally, always turn on the platform switch where one exists (e.g. Meta's disclosure tool, TikTok's AIGC label, "AI use" in YouTube Studio); the platform switch is a supplement, not a substitute for the visible label required by the AI Act, because it is not always visible at first exposure. Details: [platforms.md](platforms.md).

## Voluntary labeling

When the verdict is NO NEED, you may label voluntarily with a simple note in the description (e.g. "Graphic prepared with the help of AI"). This is an image decision, not a legal one.
